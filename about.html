Weather information made simple

WeatherWorld helps you explore current weather, air quality and sunrise and sunset times in cities around the world.

What is WeatherWorld?

WeatherWorld is a simple weather web application that lets you search for a place and view its current weather conditions. It presents useful information in one place so you can understand local conditions at a glance.

Current weather

View temperature, feels-like temperature, humidity, wind speed, precipitation and today's maximum UV index.

Air quality

Check available air quality index readings for the location you search for.

Worldwide search

Search for places by name and choose Celsius or Fahrenheit for temperature readings.

Our weather data

WeatherWorld uses Open-Meteo services for location, weather and air quality readings, plus Sunrise-Sunset.org for local sunrise and sunset times. Availability can vary by location.
